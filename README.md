# Global Electronics Retailer
## 📋 Project Overview
This is my first dashboard made using Power BI. Involves the analysis of the performance of electronics globally.

## 🛠️ Tools Used
Power BI, Power Query Editor, DAX, Charts & Slicers

## 📌 What I did?
- Cleaned the raw data using Power Query Editor            
- Created kPI cards to track business metrics-Total Sales,  No. of customers, Delivery data coverage, orders with delivery data, etc.                         
- Used Donut charts to see the total composition & contribution of online and traditional sales channel and their AOVs.                      
- Used line chart to show the total sales & number of orders on month basis               
- Visualize monthly changes in average delivery days using waterfall chart 
- Used a line & stacked column chart to show how total sales are changed by profit per unit            
- Used a funnel chart to compare the sales contribution across different countries     
- Used interactive slicers & filters for month, category and gender to enable dynamic analysis.

## 🔍 Insights
- Total sales are $55.76 millions (approx.)
- Computers generate highest sales among different product categories.
- United states is contributing the most towards sales.
- Approximately 80% of sales are traditional, indicates an opportunity to strengthen online sales. -April records the lowest sales and order volumes for all the categories, followed by a recovery in the month of May, suggests a recurring seasonal pattern in customer purchasing. Focusing on the need of an investigation in the category, AOV or sales channel.
- Sales are highly influenced by the number of orders.
- Sales and order volume are showing a significant rise, signifying the strongest performance toward year-end.
